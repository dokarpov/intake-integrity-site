# Intake Integrity — Market and design research

Updated 12 October 2026. This is a directional desk-research summary, not a customer-validation report.

## Competitive landscape

- [LeanData](https://www.leandata.com/) — lead-to-account matching, routing and revenue operations workflows. Matching itself is not a unique proposition.
- [Insycle](https://www.insycle.com/) — CRM data operations including duplicate handling. Human oversight and duplicate management are established market capabilities.
- [Openprise](https://www.openprisetech.com/) — data automation and operational workflows. Upstream data quality is a crowded category.
- [Validity DemandTools](https://www.validity.com/products/demandtools/) — data management and deduplication for CRM environments.
- [Bitrix24](https://www.bitrix24.com/) — native CRM capabilities are an important substitute and must be evaluated in customer discovery.

These are category comparisons, not a feature-by-feature verified benchmark.

## Qualitative social-listening hypotheses

Community discussions among CRM administrators and sales operators repeatedly raise concerns worth investigating: duplicate records re-entering through imports and forms; accidental merges affecting historical context; and manual workflows adding friction. Such discussions are not statistically representative. Vendor-affiliated commentary should be treated cautiously.

## Positioning hypothesis

**The right record. Or no guess.**

A pre-write identity-decision checkpoint that can abstain from ambiguous matches, route exceptions to human review, and preserve decision rationale.

This hypothesis is not proven differentiation: competitors may already support all or part of it.

## Validation work still required

1. Interview 5–8 CRM admins, sales operations leads and integrators.
2. Gather anonymized real-world ambiguous intake cases.
3. Identify whether native CRM features already prevent the error.
4. Estimate frequency and cost of incorrect assignment, review burden and integration complexity.
5. Validate a buyer, budget and willingness to pilot.

## Product claim boundaries

Current website: browser-only synthetic demonstration. No validated production matching engine, 1C connector, persistent audit trail or CRM write guarantees.
