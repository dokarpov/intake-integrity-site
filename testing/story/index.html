01 / NO SECOND INBOX

Stay where you work.

The intended integration places the finding at the existing CRM or 1C handoff. Salespeople shouldn't have to monitor another tool.

02 / RULES BEFORE GUESSWORK

Understand the discrepancy.

Identity, source, consent and candidate evidence stay visible. Shared email or phone alone isn't treated as independent proof.

03 / CONSEQUENCES RECORDED

Keep the decision trail.

A verified reviewer decision, its evidence and the original enquiry should remain traceable through controlled, idempotent delivery.

Your CRM stays the system of record.

Intake Integrity is a checkpoint before a consequential write—not a replacement CRM, monitoring dashboard or social-listening product. Real integrations and business outcomes still require validation.
