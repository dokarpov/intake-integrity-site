Privacy & data use

Staging review copy · Updated 8 November 2026

Who operates this experience?

Acorde is an independent business initiative operated by Daria Karpova in Spain. For data-access or deletion requests, use the public professional contact profile.

What does the calculator collect?

Nothing is uploaded when you edit or calculate an estimate. Your scenario inputs run in your browser. The site does not use analytics cookies or behavioral tracking scripts. When you follow a service-enquiry link, the destination receives only the service type, workflow and optional campaign-source parameters; it does not receive your numeric estimate.

What if you request a service?

Service enquiries use a separate form hosted by Tally. If you choose to submit, the form collects your business email, service interest, workflow context, optional company details, voluntary comments, consent response and referral parameters. Tally processes the submission and sends an owner notification. Please do not submit customer names, credentials, production records or sensitive personal data.

Direct email and optional updates

The workload calculator computes a scenario locally. Automatic report sending is disabled until the site operator authorizes a Google Apps Script sender under their own Google account. Once connected, submitting the report form shares your entered email and bounded scenario parameters with the operator's Google Apps Script and Google's Mail service, which sends the estimate on behalf of the operator. It does not need or collect your Gmail password. The website cannot guarantee inbox delivery.

If you independently check the optional useful-updates box and send the report, the same email contains a separate confirmation link. You are not subscribed until you open that link and explicitly confirm. The confirmation is recorded in a Google Sheet controlled by the operator with your email, timestamp, consent version and source. Future optional updates must include a working unsubscribe link. This sender is limited and not yet authorized for general public outreach; until it is connected, no recipient address or newsletter consent is transmitted by the calculator.

Purpose and legal basis

The supplied information is used to respond to your explicit request, evaluate feasibility and discuss a possible paid engagement. The legal bases are steps requested before a potential contract and, where relevant, your affirmative request for contact. It is not sold or used for unrelated marketing. Optional updates are based on your separately confirmed consent and can be withdrawn.

Retention and your rights

Enquiries are retained only while needed to handle the request or to meet applicable legal obligations; exact retention and processor settings must be verified before commercial production release. You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, as applicable, by contacting the operator. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Limits

This notice describes the calculator, the Google email sender when enabled, and the separate sales-enquiry form, not the separate authenticated CRM test backend. The staging demonstration uses fictional records and must not be used for real customer data. Tally's own data-handling and international-transfer terms are described in its privacy resources.
