Calculation method and limitations

At-risk cases = monthly requests × missed-at-entry rate + remaining requests × handoff failure rate. Potential recoverable revenue = at-risk cases × assumed sale conversion × average transaction value × potentially preventable share. Annual values multiply the monthly scenario by 12. Contribution is recoverable revenue × contribution margin. The optional rework estimate uses affected cases × potentially preventable share × staff minutes × hourly cost, and is never included in revenue.

Rates may overlap with cancellations, bad leads, capacity limits, seasonality and refunds. It does not establish causality, confirm that the transactions were profitable or predict what an implementation will recover. An audit should validate a representative sample, unique request counts, timelines, source systems, ownership and actual transaction outcomes.
