Why this matters

The cost isn't the duplicate. It's the wrong decision.

01 / IDENTITY

Shared details aren't proof.

Households, reused phone numbers and partial names can produce multiple plausible customer records.

02 / CONTROL

Automation must know when to stop.

When evidence is insufficient, an explicit hold is safer than silently choosing the closest match.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

A decision needs a reason.

Reviewers need context, options, and a trace of what was decided—not another opaque merge.

Research-informed direction

Designed around the failure, not the dashboard.

CRM matching, deduplication and review already exist. Our hypothesis is narrower: a transparent pre-write checkpoint for high-risk identity decisions, especially where incoming leads flow into legacy systems. This is a positioning hypothesis, not validated market differentiation.

COMPETITIVE CONTEXT

Matching is a mature category.

Products such as LeanData and Insycle already address matching and duplicate management. We should compete on a specific unresolved workflow, not claim to have invented matching.
